It wasn't until [the late 90s](/music/daft-punk/around-the-world) that I thought of myself as a fan of electronic music. By that point, Depeche Mode had been using their [DX7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_DX7) to churn out their brand of dark synthpop for more than a decade.

Their music is heavy with minor keys, and I love its dark and kind of off-kilter vibes. For such a popular act --- especially in the airplay-is-everything era of the 20th century --- their songs are also laden with an awful lot of overt BDSM themes!

[Personal Jesus](https://open.spotify.com/track/2wUlYDGGXlSvm2NkGj0Qio) manages this with a touch of radio-friendly subtlety, but they're also not afraid to just lay it out there on [Strangelove](https://open.spotify.com/track/6ZCyDN2ArlWB4GKAj644Cd?si=c1dfc83529c749c3) and (especially) [Master and Servant](https://open.spotify.com/track/6E6RViXGAhLXHv4Sbe8e96?si=7ad9e100549040f3).
