Yes, like everyone else and their brother, I also loved [Superman's Dead](https://open.spotify.com/track/1612JQ4JxS8bm5ky53N3bH), despite it being _horrifically_ overplayed on alt-rock radio. I bought [Clumsy](https://open.spotify.com/album/4qR92pgYZPRNV6GA9gC7OC) on CD and just about wore it out playing it so much.

Our Lady Peace draws a lot of comparisons to [The Smashing Pumpkins](https://open.spotify.com/artist/40Yq4vzPs9VNUrIBG5Jr2i), mostly because Raine Maida's kind-of-nasally vocal style is pretty similar to Billy Corgan's. I remember friends dismissing the band back in the day as "diet Smashing Pumpkins."

You want to know what I really think? _The Smashing Pumpkins are, and have always been, vastly overrated._ There, I said it.

I mean, [Bullet With Butterfly Wings](https://open.spotify.com/track/6GtX0jaNL8IjVQfrDBx81z?si=50d29e83943c4f67) is great and all, but it also felt like at some point Corgan started taking himself _just a bit_ too seriously.

By contrast, Our Lady Peace is just a little band from north of the border singing about stuff that was very relatable to a Xennial like myself. _Innocent_ was another [driving-in-my-car staple](/music/jimmy-eat-world/bleed-american) during my college years in the early 2000s.
