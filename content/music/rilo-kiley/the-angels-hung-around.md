Rilo Kiley is a fine indie band. They're okay. They'd be entirely forgettable if not for Jenny Lewis on vocals. Her voice is always drenched with emotion and her folksy-but-not-twangy delivery is just... _damn_.

Fittingly, _Under the Blacklight_ is just an okay album. [Silver Lining](https://open.spotify.com/track/0ieqq1wbtso2UjJWPqJ5Xc) is a strong opener, but on the balance there are a lot [more](https://open.spotify.com/track/7fX9QjRizVol5HIQ3ElZJM) [misses](https://open.spotify.com/track/2EPWt1wqQI17dW14jrGkix) than hits.

Rilo Kiley broke up not long after _Under the Blacklight_, and Jenny Lewis went on to do some really great solo work. I'm especially fond of [The Voyager](https://open.spotify.com/album/5sCsfubNchaI9RCpP7K7aB). Check out [She's Not Me](https://open.spotify.com/track/388mKnPdjaZ6FCRvmRmGlH) and [Just One of the Guys](https://open.spotify.com/track/5YdrHBh16li6d7U5mdTwUb).

The best Rilo Kiley songs are the ones that feel more like this later solo work, and _The Angels Hung Around_ is one of those. ([Portions for Foxes](https://open.spotify.com/track/4yY8JqTOQyi7K4O1QcQtBG), a standout track from their previous album _More Adventurous_ is another.) You might think it's just a breakup song, but then the last verse hits and it changes the meaning entirely.
