A strange thing about music is that most people simply don't pay attention to the lyrics. They'll sing along at the top of their lungs, without giving a second thought to what the artist was actually saying.

(One of my favorite examples of this: back in the FM radio days, every other day some jabroni would call in to dedicate [Lips of an Angel](https://open.spotify.com/track/40LQiUUUKXVGyNs09lHVjW) to his girlfriend. Brother, I hate to tell you, but not only is that song absolute dogshit, _it's also literally about cheating_.)

Tears for Fears is another great example of [new wave](/music/the-cure/lovesong), one of my [favorite kinds of music](/music/depeche-mode/policy-of-truth). They excel at making fun pop songs that you can sing along with, which also happen to be pretty dense with political commentary. _Everybody Wants To Rule The World_ might have been written during the last throes of the Cold War, but it's just as relevant almost 40 years later.
