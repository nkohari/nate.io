I'm a big fan of both [new wave](/music/depeche-mode/policy-of-truth) and [emo](/music/death-cab-for-cutie/i-will-follow-you-into-the-dark) music. To me, The Cure sits somewhere at the intersection.

Their music can be fun and dancy like [Friday I'm In Love](https://open.spotify.com/track/263aNAQCeFSWipk896byo6), or [Just Like Heaven](https://open.spotify.com/track/1KSkVwl54ghzE1jSbsVNBr?si=f887fe145bb74c61), or downtempo and somber like _Lovesong_. But, no matter the vibes, you can count on The Cure to deliver a catchy hook and a singable chorus.

I know they're widely considered a goth band, but to me, it seems like more of a personal style choice and it doesn't come through in their music. I mean, sure, they contributed [a song to the Crow soundtrack](https://open.spotify.com/track/0tHOTQBn2M4CD2dcyHivth), but unlike, say, [Depeche Mode](/music/depeche-mode/policy-of-truth), the undercurrent of darkness in their sound doesn't cut through the poppiness of it.
