One of my favorite 80s genres is [new wave](/music/the-cure/lovesong), which is kind of a mix of punk and proto-electronic music. I also tend to really like retrofuturism, and synthwave hits that particular mark: it combines the 80s vibe and modern synth-driven music into something brand new that somehow also sounds "vintage".

The Midnight's music is driven by a Japanese concept called [mono no aware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mono_no_aware), an appreciation of the beauty of all things, combined with a gentle sadness that everything is impermanent. With layered synths and laced with saxophone solos straight out of 80s pop radio, it somehow makes you nostalgic for arcades and 8-bit videogames.
